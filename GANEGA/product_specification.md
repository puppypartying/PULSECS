# Product Specification: PulseQC MVP

**Product Name:** PulseQC (Near Real-Time Customer Feedback Quality Control Platform)  
**Academic Context:** Universitas Padjadjaran, FEB Digital Business — Pengembangan Produk (UTS)  
**Primary Case Study Target:** Super App Polri (`superapps.polri.presisi.presisi`)  
**Architecture:** Decoupled Ingestion & Polling Worker + Full-Stack Diagnostic & QC Dashboard  

---

## 1. Product Requirements Matrix

| # | Feature | Customer Need | Pain Point | Target User | Input | Process | Output | Customer Value | Priority | Measurement Metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **F01** | **Near Real-Time Review Ingestion** | Continuous visibility into incoming customer store feedback | Manual manual scraping or checking store pages is tedious and violates policies | Operations Engineer, Product Manager | Google Play Developer API (`reviews.list`) or Historical Play Console CSV | Polling worker runs at configurable interval (default 300s); extracts user comments vs dev replies; deduplicates by `package_name + google_review_id` | Normalized review records stored in SQLite/PostgreSQL with `data_source` label | Consistent, policy-compliant pipeline without manual copy-pasting | **P1 (Core)** | Ingestion latency; duplicate error rate (0%) |
| **F02** | **In-App Diagnostic Wizard** | Clear troubleshooting for Google API & Play Console authorization | Cryptic "connection failed" errors leave teams confused about credentials | System Administrator, Technical Lead | Google Service Account JSON path, target package name | Multi-stage diagnostic check: credentials $\rightarrow$ Google API $\rightarrow$ Play Console invite $\rightarrow$ package access $\rightarrow$ review endpoint | Interactive diagnostic table with status (`PASS`/`FAIL`), failure classification, and remediation steps | Transparent setup guidance without exposing private keys | **P1 (Core)** | Time to diagnose configuration blocker |
| **F03** | **Non-Hallucinatory AI Classifier** | Standardized, objective categorization of reviews | LLMs invent non-existent server faults and produce unstructured text | QC Operator, Support Lead | Customer review text, star rating, app metadata | Gemini 3.8 Flash structured schema call with strict instructions forbidding speculative causes; extracts exact `evidence_span` | Structured classification: Sentiment, Category (14 classes), Severity, Urgency (0-100), Evidence Span | Reliable categorization backed by exact customer quotes; no black-box speculation | **P1 (Core)** | Classification accuracy vs human ground truth ($\ge 85\%$) |
| **F04** | **Deterministic Rule-Based Priority Engine** | Consistent, explainable triage ranking for complaints | Subjective operator triage leads to inconsistent handling; LLM alone is erratic | QC Operator, Triage Lead | Star rating, AI severity, urgency score, issue category, recurrence flag, cluster volume | Multi-factor weighted algorithm (0-100 score) mapping to P1, P2, P3, P4 tiers based on configurable thresholds | Priority tier (P1-P4), numeric score, and explicit human-readable `priority_reasons` | Transparent, defensible prioritization that operators can audit and trust | **P1 (Core)** | Priority assignment consistency (100% deterministic) |
| **F05** | **QC Queue & Action Workflow** | Systematic task queue to review, investigate, and resolve feedback | Unstructured spreadsheets cause duplicate work, lost complaints, and zero accountability | QC Operator, Support Agent | Ingested reviews prioritized in queue | State transition engine: `NEW` $\rightarrow$ `UNDER_REVIEW` $\rightarrow$ `INVESTIGATING` $\rightarrow$ `RESOLVED` $\rightarrow$ `CLOSED`; operator assignment and notes | Real-time queue view with dynamic SLA countdown badges and action controls | Closes the loop between customer complaints and operational remediation | **P1 (Core)** | Time from queue creation to first action |
| **F06** | **Operational Bottleneck Monitor** | Identify operational slowdowns and queue aging before escalation | No visibility into backlog age, operator overload, or systemic category bottlenecks | Operations Director, Product Lead | QC queue timestamps, case statuses, operator assignments | Computes queue age distribution, median age, oldest case, SLA breaches, operator workload concentration, category backlogs | Bottleneck dashboard with automated diagnostic warnings grounded in actual data | Proactively surfaces resource bottlenecks before service quality degrades | **P1 (Core)** | Average queue age reduction; SLA breach percentage |
| **F07** | **Semantic Issue Clustering & Trend Detection** | Detect recurring problem clusters and version-specific bug spikes | Reading reviews one-by-one obscures systemic issues affecting thousands | Product Manager, QA Engineer | Ingested reviews, detected categories, and issue descriptions | Keyword and semantic clustering; computes current vs previous period count; safe zero-baseline calculation (`NEW_ISSUE`) | Clustered issue cards showing growth percentage, trend direction, volume, and confidence | Rapid identification of post-release defects (e.g., OTP delivery failures) | **P2 (High)** | Time to identify emerging complaint spike |
| **F08** | **Human-in-the-Loop & Audit Logging** | Preserve human decision authority and accountability | Automated public actions create reputational hazards; no audit trail of overrides | Compliance Officer, Operations Lead | Operator status changes, priority overrides, severity adjustments | Immutable audit log captures `entity_type`, `entity_id`, `action`, `old_value`, `new_value`, `performed_by`, `timestamp` | Complete audit timeline visible on review detail and queue inspection | Full regulatory compliance and defensible decision traceability | **P2 (High)** | Audit trail coverage (100% of mutation events) |
| **F09** | **Research Evaluation Module (UTS Experiment)** | Scientific testing of product value proposition against manual baseline | Tech claims of "saving time" are usually unverified hype with no empirical data | Academic Researcher, Product Manager | Human ground truth labels (`true_sentiment`, `true_category`, `true_priority`), timed triage sessions | Evaluates AI prediction vs ground truth (Accuracy, Precision, Recall, Confusion Matrix); compares Control vs Treatment times | Research dashboard showing statistical time difference, F1-scores, and hypothesis test decisions | Empirical evidence for UTS examination and academic defense | **P2 (High)** | Measured time reduction percentage; F1 score |
| **F10** | **Historical CSV Export Importer** | Ingest legacy review data when live API access is restricted | Limited availability window in Google Play API and authorization bottlenecks | Data Analyst, Product Researcher | Official Google Play Console CSV export file | Parses headers, normalizes ratings and timestamps, detects duplicates, marks source as `HISTORICAL_GOOGLE_PLAY_EXPORT` | Import summary: rows detected, imported, skipped, invalid rows, average rating | Enables immediate analysis of historical review archives without live API keys | **P2 (High)** | CSV parsing success rate |
| **F11** | **Privacy Protection & Author Masking** | Protect end-user identity in operations dashboards | Displaying full customer names in internal dashboards risks privacy violations | Privacy Officer, Compliance Lead | Raw review author names | Masks names according to configurable pattern (e.g. `Budi Santoso` $\rightarrow$ `B*** S***`) | Masked author names displayed across all UI views | Compliance with data privacy principles and academic ethics | **P3 (Medium)** | Author masking enforcement (100% when enabled) |
| **F12** | **Configurable Multi-App Architecture** | Adapt platform to any Android application without code rewrite | Hard-coding package names limits product scalability beyond case study | System Administrator | App Name, Package Name, SLA Targets, Polling Interval | Dynamic database-backed system settings loaded at runtime; validated during startup | Fully reconfigured app monitoring across all pages | Enables PulseQC to serve any digital application after Super App Polri | **P3 (Medium)** | App switch time without restart |
