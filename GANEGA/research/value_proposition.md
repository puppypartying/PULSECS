# Value Proposition Statement: PulseQC

**Course:** Pengembangan Produk / Product Development (UTS)  
**Academic Institution:** Universitas Padjadjaran (FEB)

---

## 1. Formal Positioning Framework

- **FOR:** Operations Quality Control leads, Technical Support supervisors, and Product Managers of high-volume public service and enterprise mobile applications (such as Super App Polri);
- **WHO:** Struggle with high volumes of incoming app store reviews, delayed detection of critical defects, and limited human staff available to manually screen and triage feedback;
- **OUR PRODUCT (PulseQC):** Is a near real-time customer feedback quality control platform;
- **THAT PROVIDES:** Automated ingestion, non-hallucinatory AI issue classification, deterministic rule-based priority triage (P1–P4), and operational bottleneck monitoring;
- **UNLIKE:** Generic social listening tools (e.g. Brand24, Hootsuite) that focus on public relations vanity metrics, or raw Google Play Console interfaces that lack queue management and SLA tracking;
- **OUR PRODUCT:** Delivers transparent, evidence-grounded complaint prioritization that keeps human operators in control while isolating critical service-breaking defects in minutes instead of days.

---

## 2. Core Value Pillars

1. **Speed to Action (Efficiency):** Converts hundreds of unstructured reviews into an actionable queue so engineers and operators tackle P1 issues first.
2. **Defensible Accountability (Human-in-the-Loop):** Every AI prediction includes direct quote evidence spans, and every operational status mutation is immutably recorded in an audit trail.
3. **Operational Bottleneck Prevention:** Tracks queue age, median resolution latency, SLA breaches, and workload distribution to prevent queue stagnation.
4. **Academic Integrity:** Built-in research module validates workflow benefits using empirical control vs. treatment experiments.
