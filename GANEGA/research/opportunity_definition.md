# Opportunity Definition: PulseQC

**Universitas Padjadjaran — FEB Digital Business**  
**Pengembangan Produk (UTS)**

---

## 1. Product Opportunity Statement

Organizations operating essential digital mobile applications—specifically public service platforms such as Super App Polri—possess an untapped operational intelligence stream inside user app store reviews. 

The market opportunity lies in transforming **unstructured, reactive public complaints** into **structured, near-real-time actionable quality control intelligence**.

```
[ Unstructured Public Store Reviews ]
              │
              ▼
[ PulseQC Ingestion & AI Evidence Triage ]
              │
              ▼
[ Prioritized QC Queue (P1–P4) with SLA Tracking ]
              │
              ▼
[ Accelerated Operational Investigation & Bug Remediation ]
```

---

## 2. Target Market Segments

1. **Primary Segment:** Public Sector Digital Services & State-Owned Enterprise (BUMN) Applications in Indonesia.
   - *Archetypes:* Super App Polri, SATUSEHAT (Ministry of Health), PLN Mobile, Mobile JKN (BPJS Kesehatan), MyPertamina.
   - *Characteristics:* High user base ($> 1\text{M}$ downloads), continuous feedback, strict public accountability, resource-constrained internal operational triage teams.
2. **Secondary Segment:** Mid-to-Large Digital Business Applications.
   - *Archetypes:* Regional banking apps, e-commerce, fintech, logistics.
   - *Characteristics:* Rapid release cycles, high cost of unaddressed app crashes.

---

## 3. Product Differentiation Principles

- **Not a Chatbot:** PulseQC is an operational quality control system, not a conversational public-reply bot.
- **Evidence-Grounded AI:** Never claims internal technical causes without explicit quotes (`evidence_span`).
- **Deterministic Priority:** P1–P4 priority tiers are calculated via transparent mathematical rules, not erratic LLM prompts.
- **Empirical Validation:** Built-in research instrumentation designed for academic hypothesis testing.
